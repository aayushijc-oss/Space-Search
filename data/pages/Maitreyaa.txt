Maitreyaa is an upcoming Indian Marathi-language romantic drama film written and directed by Kranti Redkar and produced by Manoj Khalatkar under the banner of Samar Times Productions. The film stars Samar Khalatkar, Sky, Aayushi Bhave, and Ruchi Jail in their respective leading-role debuts.  It follows two childhood best friends whose bond is tested when new relationships enter their lives, exploring the evolving dynamics between friendship, love, and unspoken feelings.

== Production ==
Maitreyaa marks actress Kranti Redkar's second film as a director, following Kaakan (2015).


== Soundtrack ==
The music is composed by Pankajj Padghan. For the title track, the rap was written and sung by Redkar for the first time. 


== Release ==
The film was officially announced on 2 August 2026, coinciding with Friendship Day. The teaser was released on 21 September 2026 and featured the tagline, “एकीकडे प्रेम, एकीकडे मैत्री अन् एकीकडे मैत्रीत गोंधळलेलं प्रेम…” The film was initially scheduled for release on 23 October 2026 but was later postponed by a month and is scheduled to be released on 20 November 2026.
